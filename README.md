# Drip irrigation quotation checklist (India)

A short checklist for farmers, dealers and contractors who are comparing quotations for a drip irrigation system. Use it to make sure every quote lists the same things.

## 1. Source and filtration
- Water source, flow rate and pressure at the pump. Design starts here: see the [drip irrigation system design guide](https://www.blustal.com/drip-irrigation-system-design-guide/).
- Filter type and size for the water (sand, screen or disc), and how it is flushed.

## 2. Mains, sub-mains and laterals
- Pipe sizes and lengths per block, and the pressure loss the designer allowed.
- Lateral spacing matched to plant spacing.

## 3. Emitters
- Pressure-compensating emitters keep flow even on slopes and long rows: [PC dripper](https://www.blustal.com/products/micro-drip-system/pc-dripper/) and the wider [micro drip range](https://www.blustal.com/products/micro-drip-system/).
- Ask for flow per emitter at the design pressure.

## 4. Fittings, valves and fertigation
- Connectors, end caps, valves and the fertigation injector or tank.
- Automation parts, if any: [smart irrigation](https://www.blustal.com/products/smart-irrigation/).

## 5. Ask the supplier for
A bill of materials per acre, spares, and a dispatch date. Manufacturer: [Blustal](https://www.blustal.com/) (Blue Stallion Equipments Pvt Ltd, Ludhiana, since 2004, ISO 9001:2015).
